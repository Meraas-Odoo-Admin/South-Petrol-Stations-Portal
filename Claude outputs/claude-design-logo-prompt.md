**New piece for the Fuel Control System: the logo.** The system is called **"ضبط الوقود"** (tagline: "منظومة ضبط تعبئة الوقود"). It's the internal system the Sabha Security Directorate uses to account for every liter of publicly funded fuel dispensed at petrol stations: vehicles are registered at police-station desks, carry a printed card with a QR code, and every fill-up is checked against rules and logged. The logo will live in two places you've already designed: the website, and the vehicle card, where it replaces the dashed "الشعار" circle in the card's header.

**What the mark should carry:** precise, accountable control over something valuable that passes through many hands and is easy to abuse — fuel, tracked liter by liter. Territory worth exploring: a gauge's exact reading, a seal's finality, a ledger entry with a mark against it. That's a starting thought, not a brief.

**What it must avoid:**
- A generic fuel pump or fuel drop.
- A shield with a checkmark.
- Anything military or police-insignia-like: stars, eagles, wreaths, crossed swords, rank chevrons. The client already rejected an earlier name ("ضابط") because it sounded like a military rank. The mark should read as calm civic authority, not the military.
- Anything resembling the Directorate's own official emblem, Libya's state emblem, or any fuel company's logo. This is the system's own mark, not the authority's.

**Fit it into what exists — use the system's palette and type exactly:**
- Deep teal `#0D5C52` (dark `#0A4841`), amber `#E8A53D`, ink `#132524`, white.
- Cairo is the system's typeface. The Arabic wordmark "ضبط الوقود" may be custom-drawn, but it must sit comfortably next to Cairo text.

**Hard size and production constraints — these decide which ideas survive:**
- **On the card it's tiny:** the header slot is a circle **84 px across at 300 dpi — about 7 mm** — printed **white on the teal header** (which has a fine diagonal stripe texture). The symbol must still be recognizable at that size, so no thin lines or small interior details.
- **It must work as a 16 px browser favicon** and as a **phone home-screen icon** (station staff add the system to their phone's home screen).
- **Flat and solid only:** no gradients, shadows or glows. It has to survive a plastic-card printer and eventually a black-only print.
- **It must work in one color:** white on teal, teal on white, and pure black on white.

**Process:** first show **three clearly different directions**, each as symbol plus wordmark, each shown at card size (7 mm), favicon size and large, so we can pick one. Then develop the chosen direction into the full set below.

**Final set, for the chosen direction:**
1. Symbol alone.
2. Arabic wordmark alone.
3. Horizontal lockup (symbol beside wordmark) for the website sidebar and header, laid out right-to-left.
4. Stacked lockup (symbol above wordmark) for the login screen.
5. One-color versions: white, teal `#0D5C52`, and pure black `#000000`.
6. App icon: the symbol on a teal square, with the symbol kept inside the central safe area, so phones that crop icons to a circle don't clip it.
7. A small rules sheet: minimum size, and the clear space to keep around the mark.
8. **In context:** the website sidebar (replacing the current text-only name), the login screen, the vehicle card front at real size with the mark in the header circle, a phone home screen showing the app icon, and a browser tab showing the favicon.

Every final version must be pure vector shapes, so it can be exported as SVG and used unchanged on the website and in the card's print file.
